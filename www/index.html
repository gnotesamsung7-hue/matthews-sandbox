🎙️ Sound studio

Record your own voice for the game! Tap ⏺ to record (up to 3 seconds) and tap ⏹ to stop. Pick a voice: 🙂 normal, 🐿️ chipmunk or 👹 monster.

How to play

Spawn: pick a tab (People, Things, Weapons), pick something, then tap the scene to drop it.

Weapons: drop one on a dummy's hand and it holds it. Poke the weapon (or the dummy) to use it.

Make your own character: People → ➕ New. Tap your character twice to change it.

📡 Play together: both phones need the app (APK) and the same Wi-Fi (or one phone's hotspot). One phone taps Host, the other taps Join.

🪙 Coins: win them in battles, Hide and Seek and star goals, then spend them in the 🛒 Shop (Tools tab).

⚔️ Battle: pick a level, 1 or 2 players, a hero and a superpower. Beat 3 waves of baddies, then the boss! Two players share one screen (or a keyboard: P1 = A D W F, P2 = arrows and L).

Tools: Magnet pulls, Wind blows, Grow and Shrink resize, Copy makes twins. 🗺️ picks a stage, 🎙️ lets you record your own sounds.

Drag throws things around. Poke tickles dummies, squeaks ducks, lights TNT, pops balloons and starts carts.

Goals: the yellow bar shows a fun thing to try. Finish it to win a star. Tap it to hear it again. 🎁 drops a surprise, 🧪 changes the place.

Rope: tap one thing, then another (or empty space to tie it to the wall). Tie balloons to a dummy and watch it float.

Pin freezes a part in place, Boom blasts anywhere, Heal wakes a knocked-out dummy, Delete removes things.

Dummies get dizzy from hard hits and go floppy when knocked out.
