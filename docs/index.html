How to play

Spawn: pick something in the bottom row, then tap the scene to drop it. Tap again to drop more.

Drag throws things around. Use lights TNT, pops balloons, starts carts and makes dummies jump.

Rope: tap one thing, then another (or empty space to tie it to the wall). Tie balloons to a dummy and watch it float.

Pin freezes a part in place, Boom blasts anywhere, Heal wakes a knocked-out dummy, Delete removes things.

Dummies get dizzy from hard hits and go floppy when knocked out.
